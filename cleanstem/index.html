FAQ

Everything you need to know before ordering.

What audio formats do you accept?

We accept WAV, MP3, AAC, FLAC, and AIFF. For best results, send lossless files (WAV/FLAC) at 44.1kHz or higher.

How fast is turnaround?

Single stems: 2–4 hours. Full splits: 4–8 hours. Bulk projects are scoped individually. Rush delivery available on request.

What quality can I expect?

Studio-grade isolation using spectral AI models. Vocals are clean enough for remixing, drums are tight, and bass retains low-end integrity. We do a manual QC pass on every delivery.

Can I use the stems commercially?

Absolutely. You retain full rights to your original material and the separated stems. No usage restrictions.
